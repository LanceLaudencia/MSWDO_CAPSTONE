About DSWD & MSWDO

The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) is the primary government agency in the Philippines for social protection and poverty-reduction programs.

The Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) ensures national programs are implemented effectively at the municipal level.

DSWD outreach and on-site assistance activities for vulnerable communities.

Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office serving local beneficiaries.

DSWD Programs Implemented by MSWDO

AICS

Provides financial, medical, burial, transportation, and food assistance to crisis-affected families.

SEA-K

Livelihood capital and skills training for poor families to establish sustainable income projects.

Senior & PWD Assistance

Social pension, benefits, and support services for senior citizens and persons with disabilities.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Vision: Empowered communities with improved quality of life.

Mission: Lead the formulation and coordination of social welfare programs.

Core Values: Maka-Diyos, Maka-Tao, Makabayan, at Makakalikasan.
