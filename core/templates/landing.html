About the System

The MSWDO Management System is a centralized platform designed to support the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office in efficiently managing, monitoring, and delivering social services. It promotes transparency, reduces paperwork, and ensures that assistance reaches those who need it most.

Our Services

Self Employment Assistance (SEA)

Provision of capital assistance to individuals or groups engaged in small-scale businesses.

Aid to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS)

Temporary assistance for families facing economic difficulties or emergencies.

Socorro Health Care Card

Unified health care benefits for indigents and government employees.

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Vision: A municipality of self-reliant and empowered people whose rights are protected and potentials developed.

Mission: To uplift the well-being of the poor and disadvantaged through social programs and empowerment.

Objectives: Strengthen community participation and improve quality of life.
