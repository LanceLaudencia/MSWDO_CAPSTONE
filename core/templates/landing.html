About Us

About DSWD & MSWDO

Committed to serving our communities with dedication and transparency

The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) is the primary government agency in the Philippines for social protection and poverty-reduction programs.

The Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) ensures national programs are implemented effectively at the municipal level, bringing services directly to communities.

Our Services

DSWD Programs Implemented by MSWDO

Comprehensive social welfare programs designed to uplift communities

AICS

Provides financial, medical, burial, transportation, and food assistance to crisis-affected families.

SEA-K

Livelihood capital and skills training for poor families to establish sustainable income projects.

Senior & PWD Assistance

Social pension, benefits, and support services for senior citizens and persons with disabilities.

Vision

Empowered communities with improved quality of life through inclusive and sustainable social welfare programs.

Mission

Lead the formulation and coordination of social welfare programs that promote social development and protection.

Core Values

Maka-Diyos, Maka-Tao, Makabayan, at Makakalikasan - guiding principles in all our services.
