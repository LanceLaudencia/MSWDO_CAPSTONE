About Us

About MSWDO

Committed to serving our communities with dedication and transparency

The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) is the primary government agency in the Philippines for social protection and poverty-reduction programs.

The Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) ensures national programs are implemented effectively at the municipal level, bringing services directly to communities.

Our Services

DSWD Programs Implemented by MSWDO

Comprehensive social welfare programs designed to uplift communities

AICS

AICS provides immediate, short-term support to individuals and families facing unforeseen crises — illness, death, natural disasters, accidents, and loss of income.

SEA

A livelihood and capability-building program providing capital, skills training, and entrepreneurial support to poor and disadvantaged families.

Red Card

A quick-access assistance card issued to qualified beneficiaries, enabling expedited support for urgent needs without repeating the full application process each time.

Educational Assistance

Financial support for tuition, school supplies, and related expenses to help students from low-income families continue and complete their education.
