AICS

Ayuda sa Indibidwal na nasa Krisis na Sitwasyon

AICS provides immediate financial assistance to individuals and families in crisis situations who are unable to meet their basic needs due to sudden or unexpected circumstances.

  • Who can apply: Indigent individuals or families facing medical, death, fire, calamity, or other crisis situations.
  • Type of aid: Financial assistance for medical bills, funeral expenses, food, transportation, or other immediate needs.
  • Requirements: Valid ID, proof of crisis (medical certificate, death certificate, etc.), and barangay certification.
  • Note: One-time grant per crisis situation, subject to available funds and social worker assessment.

SEA — Kaunlaran

Self-Employment Assistance — Kaunlaran

SEA-K is a livelihood assistance program that provides capital assistance to indigent individuals who wish to start or expand a small business to achieve economic independence.

  • Who can apply: Indigent individuals 18 years and above with a viable livelihood or small business plan.
  • Type of aid: Seed capital or livelihood starter kit (sari-sari store, vending, farming, etc.).
  • Requirements: Valid ID, proof of indigency, business proposal, and barangay certification.
  • Note: Beneficiaries undergo livelihood skills training before release of assistance.

RED CARD

Rapid Emergency Disaster Card for Affected Residents

The Red Card program provides immediate relief assistance to Socorro residents affected by natural or man-made disasters such as fires, floods, or typhoons.

  • Who can apply: Residents of Socorro directly affected by a disaster or emergency incident.
  • Type of aid: Food packs, non-food items, temporary shelter assistance, and cash aid.
  • Requirements: Disaster/incident report from barangay, valid ID, and proof of residency.
  • Note: Priority given to most vulnerable — elderly, PWD, pregnant women, and children.
MSWDO — Socorro Municipality
Educational Assistance

Municipal Educational Assistance Program

The Educational Assistance Program supports indigent students of Socorro by providing financial assistance for tuition fees, school supplies, and other educational expenses to help them continue their studies.

  • Who can apply: Indigent students from elementary to college level who are residents of Socorro.
  • Type of aid: Financial assistance for tuition, school supplies, uniforms, and other educational needs.
  • Requirements: Valid ID, certificate of enrollment, report card, proof of indigency, and barangay certification.
  • Note: Students must maintain good academic standing and reapply each school year.
